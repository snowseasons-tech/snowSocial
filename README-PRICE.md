# SnowSocial MVP Pricing Recommendation

**Prepared:** November 3, 2026  
**Market:** One small business, one brand, up to five connected social profiles

## Recommended offer: managed social media service

Sell the MVP as a managed service powered by SnowSocial. This price covers the work of planning and publishing content; the software is the workflow behind the service.

| Charge | Price | Scope |
|---|---:|---|
| Onboarding and setup | **$350 once** | Connect up to five profiles (one each for Facebook, Instagram, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, and X), verify permissions, set up the first content calendar, test publishing, and provide a walkthrough |
| Monthly service | **$1,000/month** | Up to 12 original post concepts per month, adapted captions for connected channels, simple branded graphics, client approval, scheduling, and a monthly performance summary with CSV export |
| Six-month prepay | **$5,700** | 5% off the monthly service portion; equivalent to $950/month |
| Twelve-month prepay | **$10,800** | 10% off the monthly service portion; equivalent to $900/month |

Prepaid discounts apply to the service portion only; bill the setup fee separately. Collect monthly service fees in advance.

### Define one post clearly

One post means one original content idea, with copy and a simple graphic where appropriate. Reformatting or lightly adapting that approved post for the connected channels does not use additional post credits. This keeps the 12-post allowance understandable while allowing cross-posting.

### Keep outside the base package

Quote these separately:

- Video, Reels, on-site photography, and substantial custom design
- Paid campaign management and advertising spend
- Daily comment, review, or inbox management
- Additional original posts beyond the monthly allowance
- Major revisions after content approval

Charge third-party platform/API usage at cost or pass through the provider's billed usage. In particular, X analytics is disabled by default because API reads are metered.

## Optional software-only pilot

If the business will create and approve its own content, offer a limited **$99/month** software pilot plus **$250 one-time onboarding**. This should cover one business workspace, up to five connected profiles, scheduled publishing, monthly analytics, CSV export, hosting, and basic support. It does not include writing, graphics, or ongoing account management.

Keep this as a direct pilot for one client. The current MVP is single-workspace and single-admin; it is not yet a self-serve multi-client SaaS product.

## Why these prices are reasonable

Software-only products are much cheaper than a managed service: Buffer lists its Essentials plan at $5 per channel per month; Later lists Starter at $18.75 per month billed yearly for one social set with eight profiles; and Sprout Social lists Essentials at $79 per seat per month billed annually for five social profiles. Those plans are software comparisons, not like-for-like managed-service offers.

A managed package costs more because it includes content planning, creation, adaptation, approvals, scheduling, and reporting. Sprout Social's overview places freelancer social media management broadly in the $500–$3,000 monthly range. The recommended $1,000 price is an estimate for the defined workload above, not a guarantee of a particular sales or engagement result.
