Social account access & publishing consent

This form records permission for Snow Seasons Tech to use SnowSocial with the specific business accounts listed below. Platform authorization is completed separately through each platform's official connection flow.

Printable consent form. This page does not send or save the information entered here. Print or save a completed copy, sign it, and return it to the SnowSocial workspace administrator.

2. Accounts covered by this authorization

Select only the business profiles you authorize. Enter the profile or page name and, if available, its URL or provider account ID.

3. What you authorize

For the selected accounts, I authorize Snow Seasons Tech and its SnowSocial service to:

  • Connect to the selected profiles through the platform's authorization flow and use the granted permissions to operate the service.
  • Prepare and edit social post drafts, schedule them, and publish them only after I or an authorized workspace administrator approves them in SnowSocial.
  • Access performance information the platforms make available for published posts and use it to prepare monthly summaries and post-level reports.

Publishing formats and available metrics depend on the platform. SnowSocial currently supports Facebook text posts, Instagram single-image posts, Google Business Profile Local Posts, LinkedIn organization text posts, and X text posts. X analytics is optional and may incur provider usage charges if enabled.

4. What is not authorized

This consent does not authorize paid advertising or ad spend; reading or sending direct messages; replying to comments; changing account ownership, security settings, or profile information; deleting existing posts; or publishing content that has not been approved in SnowSocial.

5. Access, security, and withdrawal

  • SnowSocial uses the platform's authorization flow; it does not ask for the platform account password. Provider access tokens are stored encrypted at rest.
  • You may withdraw access by revoking SnowSocial's authorization in the platform's connected-app settings and asking the workspace administrator to cancel future scheduled posts.
  • Revoking access can stop future publishing and reporting. Posts already published remain on the platform unless you remove them separately.

6. Confirmation and signature

This authorization covers only the selected profiles and activities described above. It does not replace the platform's own permission screen or any separate service agreement.
